A couple of weeks ago I did a talk at [Hacker News London](http://www.meetup.com/HNLondon/) on the Story of [Dataloop.IO](www.dataloop.io) and how we got our initial customers and traction using Customer Development.

<div class="embed"><iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/110478908" title="Vimeo video" loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

[David Gideh – Using Customer Development to get Traction in a Crowded Space](https://vimeo.com/110478908) from [HN London](https://vimeo.com/hnlondon) on [Vimeo](https://vimeo.com).

In this talk I share:

-   What we did different at Dataloop.IO from my first two SaaS businesses to get to 6-figure revenue in just 2 months of founding the company
-   How we did our customer development to validate our idea with potential customers
-   How we turned our customer development into content that got us initial traction, credibility and customers

You can see the original blog referred to in the talk here: [https://blog.dataloop.io/2014/01/30/what-we-learnt-talking-to-60-companies-about-monitoring/](https://blog.dataloop.io/2014/01/30/what-we-learnt-talking-to-60-companies-about-monitoring/)
